# Picture map

`assets/forest-friends/world-map.webp` is painted from the world itself by `tools/map/map.html`
(covers `MAP` in `js/friends/world/big/layout.js`; pins are placed from the places' coordinates in
`js/friends/game.js`). After changing the world, serve the repository root and run `node tools/map/render.cjs`.

The painter draws a storybook map on paper: the land as a soft wash of the world's own ground colours lit from the north-west, the Rock's beds and the snow, meadow flowers and swamp reeds; water drawn sharp with a light shore, a dark outline and little waves; dotted earth trails; every tree as a small drawing seen from the south; the places as small drawings (den tree, bridges, dam and lodge, cave mouth and its way to the hall, summit cross, well, picnic fire, signposts, toadstool house and ring, stone circle, apple trees, berry bushes, sett, the Steinbock); the wood running out into the paper at both sides, a compass and the name on a ribbon. Default size 1800 × 1200 (`?s=` pixels per metre).
