Strengthen sculpted volumes, non-flat terrain, visible thickness/side faces, directional sunlight, contact shadows, coherent material highlights and layered distance. Avoid both flat illustrative treatment and the v5 clay-blob simplification. This remains an art-direction still, not a playable 3D engine or validated performance target.

The accepted den/fox, v5 compact shop layout and automatic answer-to-next-task flow remain. Frozen v1/v2 references and earlier revision files are preserved.

Prompt: restore richer v4 world, preserve topology and character identity, strengthen solid modelled geometry, terrain and volumetric lighting/materials, no UI/text; retain medium game-readable detail rather than excessive foliage microtexture. Store the new WebP as a sibling asset.
