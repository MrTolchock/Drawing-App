# Design review v4 — 7 November 2026

Current review is review-v4.html and the private Forest friends Design reference Site. Sites is the maintained viewing destination. Frozen v1/v2 references and their manifest remain untouched; review-v3.html is preserved as an earlier working revision.

## Decisions

- Every puzzle submits on the answer action directly. No checkmark/confirm button in Maus, counting or future puzzle screens. For construction/drag tasks, the final placement is the answer action; no redundant confirmation. Keep immediate non-punitive feedback and a separate next-task arrow. Progress marks are feedback, not submission controls.
- Replace the puzzle success check icon with a friendly smile to avoid confusing feedback with a confirmation button. The shop still confirms a purchase because spending is a separate action.
- Illustrate the shop as a cosy tree interior following the accepted den art. Keep shelf bays uncluttered for selectable objects. Click/tap an object to reveal its price beside the wallet.
- Show each resource as its own row. One visible token equals one owned unit: three logs are three log pictures, two nuts are two nut pictures, and so on. Prices use the same representation. No numeric multiplier or numeric bag badge. Screen-reader names and speech retain quantities. A zero balance is an empty dashed tray. Large future wallets require a reviewed grouping/scroll treatment; the current sample is deliberately small.
- Raise the forest camera to an elevated, downward-looking three-quarter angle. Preserve larger, closer objects than the Emberwick reference rather than adopting its distant bird perspective. New forest-v4.webp is an illustration study: den, shop, brook and bridge retain their topology; actual camera pitch and navigation require a 3D review.

## Assets
