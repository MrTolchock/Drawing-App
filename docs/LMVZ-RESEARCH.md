# Official-source exercise research and original Farbtupf activities

Reviewed 4 November 2026 using the browser plugin. The earlier LMVZ access limitation in RESEARCH.md is resolved for the public pages below. This report supplements the existing handwriting research; it does not replace it.

## Scope and evidence

Farbtupf is a first-grade app. Research included later primary and secondary materials for breadth, but their subject matter is not automatically suitable for first graders. All new prompts, datasets, game rules, SVG shapes and missions are original. No textbook scans, publisher characters, proprietary worksheets, audio, or illustrations are bundled. Public product descriptions describe teaching approaches; they do not expose the complete licensed exercise banks. Those were not accessed.

The browser review covered the following public primary sources. Summaries describe what was actually visible; proposed app adaptations are our own decisions.

## Original implemented exercise families

Each adventure has three freely selectable stages and five tasks per round. The existing trimester labels remain a provisional app recommendation, not a publisher or canton timetable. New adventure screens use “Stufe” to describe difficulty.

| Adventure | Interaction | Progression | Reflection/transfer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sternenrakete | Tap empty places to compose the target quantity | Totals up to 5 / 10 / 20 | Describe a different decomposition |
| Waldpicknick | Distribute individual berries to two plates; undo and check | Up to 6 / 10 / 20 berries | Equal amounts, then discuss different needs |
| Zauberzwillinge | Recognise two equal star groups and choose the total | Doubles up to 6 / 10 / 20 | Make equal finger pictures |
| Formenwerkstatt | Select original SVG shapes | Names / corners / rotated shapes | Find shapes nearby; square as a special rectangle |
| Reimzirkus | Speak or listen to words, choose a rhyme | Three banks of five pairs | Invent a silly sentence |
| Silbentrommel | Tap a drum once per spoken syllable; undo and check | One–two / up to three / up to four syllables | Tap the child's own name |
| Roboterpost | Use directional buttons to travel around obstacles | 3×3 / 4×4 / 5×5 grids, changing start/goal | Explain or compare routes |
| Was kommt dann? | Select picture/text steps in order; reset and check | Two / three / four steps | Tell or draw a related story |

There are **18 creative missions**, six per app stage, spanning art, mathematics, language, observation and social reasoning. Each has three steps, support, reflection and an optional extension. They open the existing painting tools with a visible task reminder. The current drawing is retained; the child can choose a new sheet. The mission tick is self-reflection, not automatic artistic or ethical assessment. Drawing rewards retain the existing rules.

## Limits and age-sensitive decisions

- Later-grade sources are explicitly distinguished from first-grade source material. Handwriting, fractions, French, English and AI progression were not inferred from general product marketing.
- The app has no publisher affiliation or official approval. It supplements classroom teaching.
- Rhyme and syllable tasks need a spoken model when children cannot yet read the words. The app says to speak aloud or ask someone to read; it provides no recorded listening instruction or pronunciation scoring.
- Nature timelines describe specific small scenarios and approximate processes. Observation missions connect the screen to real surroundings. Quizzes alone do not establish inquiry competence.
- Shapes include a square and a visibly elongated rectangle. Four-corner prompts avoid presenting both as competing uniquely correct answers. Rotation preserves the shape.
- Adult support is appropriate for instructions and reflection. No automatic grading of open drawings or opinions, timers, public rankings or penalties were added.
- All runtime assets are local and included in the service worker. No external images or source-site requests are needed to play.

## Verification

Run `node tests/learning.test.cjs` and `node tests/adventures.test.cjs`. These check the existing games, offline asset coverage, storage compatibility, new answer/range invariants, reachable robot routes and complete mission content. Browser checks cover the visible interactions separately; neither automated checks nor browser play certify educational effectiveness or physical iPad compatibility.

Results for this change:

- All 39 automated tests passed. Every new game/stage combination was checked over 40 seeded runs of five tasks, including robot-route reachability.
- The browser plugin completed all 24 new rounds (120 tasks) through visible controls, followed by a five-task replay. The replay retained 24 stars instead of awarding another one.
- Checked rocket retries/toggles, berry undo, syllable undo, story reset, robot obstacles and backtracking, rotated shapes, mission filtering (18 total), the painting reminder dialog and returning to an existing drawing.
- Checked saved stars and mission reflection after reload. Stopped the local web server, then reloaded successfully and opened the new mission content, verifying service-worker delivery while the origin was unavailable.
- Visually reviewed 390×844 phone and 820×1180 tablet layouts; no horizontal document overflow. A browser drag produced a stroke which survived leaving and reopening the painting activity. Browser error log was empty. These were browser clicks/drag and viewport checks, not tests on a physical iPad or synthetic touch-event coverage.
