# Curriculum references — current puzzles

All 34 current puzzles are mapped individually in the [generated catalogue](PUZZLE-CATALOGUE.md), with a bounded competence, difficulty, prerequisites and every iteration/solution. That inventory defines the implemented content. The [former planning matrix](../archive/design-history/forest-friends/curriculum-matrix.md) is archived; its five-puzzle milestone and backlog do not describe the current game.

| Practice area | Official Zürich Zyklus-1 reference | Limits of the app activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Numerals and quantities | [MA.1.A.2](https://zh.lehrplan.ch/index.php?code=a%7C5%7C0%7C1%7C1%7C2) | Pictured counting and neighbouring numbers practise bounded ranges; see each puzzle for the actual range |
| Addition, completing, doubling and sharing | [MA.1.A.3](https://zh.lehrplan.ch/index.php?code=a%7C5%7C0%7C1%7C1%7C3) | Correct tapping/counting alone does not establish non-counting calculation strategies or reasoning |
| Forms, positions and symmetry | [MA.2.A.1](https://zh.lehrplan.ch/index.php?code=a%7C5%7C0%7C2%7C1%7C1) and related form/position steps on each puzzle page | Matching and constructing practise parts of the stated step, not the whole competence |
| Small pictured surveys | [MA.3.C.1](https://zh.lehrplan.ch/index.php?code=a%7C5%7C0%7C3%7C3%7C1) | Reading/comparing small counts is narrower than designing and interpreting a real survey |
| Familiar words and sentence meaning | [D.2.A.1](https://zh.lehrplan.ch/index.php?code=a%7C1%7C11%7C2%7C1%7C1), [D.2.B.1](https://zh.lehrplan.ch/index.php?code=a%7C1%7C11%7C2%7C2%7C1) | Requires introduced letters and familiar vocabulary; picture-only or memorized success does not prove decoding |
| Letter/digit forms | [D.4.A.1](https://zh.lehrplan.ch/index.php?code=a%7C1%7C11%7C4%7C1%7C1) | Finger tracing does not assess pencil grip, independent writing, fluency or posture |
| Animals, visible traits and habitats | [NMG.2.4](https://zh.lehrplan.ch/index.php?code=a%7C6%7C1%7C2%7C0%7C4) and related steps on puzzle pages | Written riddles also require reading; real observation and conversation remain necessary |

Zyklus 1 spans kindergarten and the first two primary years. App ranges are design choices for first-grade practice, not official yearly requirements. References describe opportunities to practise, not mastery, certification or official approval. Memory/sequence enrichment must not receive an invented attainment claim.

The owner's [individual review](PUZZLE-REVIEW.md) checks actual wording, answer, picture clarity, difficulty and suitability. New or changed content/difficulty requires review again. Original source investigations are retained in [Research](RESEARCH.md).
