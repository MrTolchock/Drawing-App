# Der Herzbaum — story, gameplay and visual design

The complete collected Heart Tree design pack, including all earlier options,
selected references, interaction specifications, review PDFs and implementation
captures. [All design work](../README.md) · [All visualisations](GALLERY.md).

**Selected direction: original A, Painted woodland**, the left column of the
[A/B comparison](visual-middle-ground-options.png). The story uses a connected
woodland loop, short complete quest arcs and the chapters **Meet → Reconnect →
Gather**. The child is 6–7 and initially cannot read. The forest cast and flora
follow the Swiss-native direction established during design.

The complete playable story and the painted presentation are implemented. See
[current app notes](../../docs/HEART-TREE.md), [painted app captures](painted-implementation/README.md)
and the [private playable preview](https://farbtupf-herzbaum-preview-k7m4.andreas-itten.chatgpt.site).
Earlier plans and screenshots are historical records; their statements about
implementation, approval or future work describe their original stage.
