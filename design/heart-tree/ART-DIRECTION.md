## Carry these qualities into production

- Warm ivory light, soft yellow-sage and forest greens, dusty blue water and a clear orange fox accent.
- Broad painted canopy masses, softly modelled trunks and roots, restrained natural texture and simple rounded stones.
- Generous quiet ground between landmarks, clearly continuous sandy paths and sparse low vegetation. Keep the original A balance of detail and open space.
- Details grouped locally around a resident, bank or tree base. Characters, paths and meaningful objects remain easy to distinguish.
- Expressive orange fox with green neckerchief and cream muzzle/tail tip. Residents share its warmth and simple readable silhouettes.
- A more symbolic overview map and a volumetric close world, with coherent colours, shapes and landmarks between them.

## Map and world responsibilities

The map communicates the Heart Tree, five clearings, main loop and restored connections. The future Three.js world gives these places depth, movement and local interaction. It uses actual 3D terrain, props and characters with a fixed angled overhead orthographic camera, approximately 55–60 degrees down.

Keep the fox visible, fade obstructing foreground foliage, and preserve clear landing space for taps near the character and residents. Use a local picture or meaningful gesture for an invitation. Extra puzzle collections stay within familiar places and the picture-led book rather than adding visible objects across the whole map.

Animation supports actions: walking, a resident's gesture, a bridge repair and a brief tree response. Quiet areas remain quiet when nothing needs attention.

The existing flat puzzle artwork retains its current visual treatment. The first-quest storyboard illustrates guidance and return behaviour; final activity assignment and individual puzzle review remain separate work.

## Swiss-native palette

The Heart Tree is a pedunculate oak. Supporting woodland forms are beech, hazel and alder, with appropriate moss, wild strawberry and common reeds near water. Residents remain Eurasian beaver, European brown hare, European hedgehog, tawny owl and common kingfisher. Preserve the brown hare's long black-tipped ears, the beaver's broad tail and the tawny owl's round head.

## What still needs to be demonstrated

The selected look is approved as a visual direction. Its exact 3D assets, performance, touch readability and effectiveness for children require a later playable slice and observation on the intended device. The reference images are concept illustrations.
