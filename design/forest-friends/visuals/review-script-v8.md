# Child and teacher review — version 8

Record date, review revision, viewport/device, adult help given, observation and proposed change. Use anonymous child labels only. Do not label task content approved until the teacher review is recorded.

## Child walkthrough

1. Start in the den. Without explaining the icons, observe how the child leaves, chooses a pictured friend and replays speech. Note every adult prompt.
2. Blaumeise: watch the separate example, replay target/choice words and try one scored task. Ask the child to describe what is being matched, without prompting the rhyme answer. Observe accidental taps on answer versus audio.
3. Waldkauz: listen to the story and place the three cards. Observe left-to-right understanding and whether the child can undo a card. Leave halfway, return and check that the existing arrangement is recognisable.
4. Biber: use the separate example, select/place pieces and undo a misplaced piece. Observe the target outline, unused grid cells and whether fixed piece orientation is understood.
5. Complete a five-task round. Observe whether the child recognises thanks, three resource tokens and transfer to the bag. Return to the reward screen and check that the balance stays unchanged.
6. Shop: choose the cushion. Ask the child to show its price and the contents of their bag. Observe comparison of one row per resource, purchase intention and return home. Do not use this sample starting wallet to approve production balance.

Record independent / after replay / after example / after adult help for each step. Mistakes are observations, not a child score. Any repeated adult explanation is an interface revision candidate.

## Teacher review

- Confirm the matrix mappings and concrete first-grade prerequisites. Zyklus 1 is not itself a first-grade sequence.
- Sound D.1.A.1.b: vocabulary, fixed Swiss Standard German pronunciation, unique rhyme, Stall/Traum image recognisability.
- Story D.1.B.1.b: distinct actions and comprehension of the heard narrative, including the bell/wave/book case where everyday logic alone does not determine order.
- Space MA.2.A.2.b composition part: suitable piece/target range, fixed orientation, valid permutations, and whether colours or positions give away an answer.
- Reading/counting: introduced letters/number ranges, syllable bows and absence of automatically spoken scored reading answers.
- Review every added task pool before marking 15 items/variants accepted; the five specimens are not a release pool.
