# Revision 9 review script

1. Ask the child to find two Reading friends using portraits. Confirm category names are helpful but not needed for navigation.
2. Blaumeise: watch the word/picture example, then choose a written rhyme. Note whether the child reads, uses pictures, or requests audio; do not infer decoding from the answer alone.
3. Waldkauz: use the separate sleeping-mouse example. Let the child read Die Maus sitzt. and choose between sitting/running. Check taught letters, verb comprehension and whether illustrations distinguish actions. Do not speak the target unless recording answer-revealing help. No countdown.
4. Biber: find the missing shape. Observe whether one tap and the filled gap are understood; recognition alone does not prove naming/composition.
5. Try a correction, leave/return and complete a five-task round. Confirm only one reward credit. Check wallet, shop and home continuity separately from this disposable prototype.
6. Teacher: review the exact letter inventory, three-word sentence range, support policy, rhyme uniqueness and geometry warm-up against the matrix. Expand/review pools before release; physical touch testing remains needed.

Record actual observations and requested help. The user's approval of examples and categories is design feedback; teacher and child results remain pending.
