# Design revision 5 — 7 November 2026

Current working version: review-v5.html and the existing private Forest friends Site. Previous versioned references remain intact. No runtime game files changed.

## Accepted feedback carried into this review

- All puzzle answer actions submit immediately. For counting and Maus, a correct tap shows success, then moves on automatically after a short 1.4-second feedback pause. Remove both confirm and next-task controls; incorrect responses stay available for retry. No punitive countdown. Progress uses filled dots rather than checkmarks. Separate worked examples and spoken directions remain.
- The more abstract world uses broad tree-canopy shapes, simple materials, fewer flowers and foliage clusters, clean river/terrain and softly rounded geometry. This is a visual target for a feasible realtime 3D world, not evidence of a model budget, playable engine or tested rendering performance. The fox's established colours, silhouette and green neckerchief remain the character direction; frozen character and den references are unchanged.
- The shop uses the same simpler art direction. Its shelf scene and compact price/wallet dock are separate layout regions, so panels cannot cover the shelf. The dock is 154 CSS pixels high on desktop and 143 at phone width. Keep individual token rows; no numeric shorthand.
- Purchase confirmation is still deliberate and distinct from puzzle submission.

## Verification

Squirrel: selecting 7 immediately succeeds and automatically shows task 2 with four nuts. Maus: selecting the mouse immediately succeeds and automatically shows Ha-se. Neither has a confirm or next control. Browser review at 390 CSS pixels verifies no horizontal overflow, no scene/dock overlap, and one token row each for three logs, three nuts and two feathers. This does not replace physical iPhone/iPad testing.

## New art

forest-v5.webp and shop-v5.webp are new revisions created with the built-in image tool from the v4 images. Prompts: preserve topology, elevated camera, large close landmarks and warm identity, but simplify into chunky rounded geometry, flat colours, broad foliage masses, sparse plants and no microtexture; apply the same simplification to the shop with large empty shelf bays and no UI baked into the image. WebP conversion only; original generated PNGs are retained in this conversation.

Teacher/child review, full content pools, actual 3D geometry/camera/performance and production continuity remain outstanding.
