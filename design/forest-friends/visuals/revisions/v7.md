# Design review v7 — 7 November 2026

The user selected the accepted fox den as the master style for all screens and specified the rendering split: outdoors, den and shop are 3D; puzzles are 2D. See [shared art direction](../art-direction.md).

## Changes

- New forest-v7.webp uses den-v1.png as the style anchor and v6 exterior as layout reference. Softer coherent wood/terrain, warm cream highlights, gentler light, natural green foliage, rounded character proportions and reduced microdetail now follow the den.
- New shop-v7.webp preserves empty display bays and adopts the den's warm organic roots, terracotta, softly textured timber and window light.
- New transparent shop-goods-v7.webp contains a dimensional cushion and apple. The design review crops the two cells with CSS; these are render studies, not meshes. Wallet and price tokens remain readable 2D overlays.
- Restyled flat 2D puzzle cards, progress, dialogue/demo panels, map and drawing palette share warm cream, honey wood and leaf-green colours. Keep the original character sheet and den artwork untouched.
- Existing single-tap/automatic puzzle flow, syllable bows and compact non-overlapping shop dock are retained.

## Art method and prompts

Built-in image generation: (1) den as master style plus exterior topology as input, preserve landmarks/camera and fox identity; (2) den as master style plus shop layout, warm textured roots/timber/light, empty shelves and no UI; (3) transparent two-cell sheet of a honey cushion and red apple, softly rendered in den style. Original generated PNGs remain in the creating conversation; versioned WebP assets are project copies. Alpha transparency was verified for goods.
