# Shared art direction — den as master reference

**Current decision: 7 November 2026.** The accepted original den, `den-v1.png`, and its Fuchs are the primary visual anchor. Preserve the original den and character sheet without alteration. Use them to judge every new scene, asset and interface revision.

## Rendering split required for development

| Area | Production presentation | Shared style |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outdoors | Navigable 3D geometry, characters, camera and lighting | Soft sculpted woodland forms, dimensional characters, natural warm materials, gentle light and contact shadows matching the den |
| Fuchs den | 3D interior with interactive objects | Preserve accepted composition, fox proportions, green neckerchief, rounded roots, warm wood, terracotta and cream |
| Shop | 3D interior and selectable dimensional goods | Same wood, rounded forms, soft texture, light and character-scale objects as den; keep shelves visible |
| Learning puzzles | Flat 2D scenes/cards, illustrations and inputs | Related warm palette, rounded shapes, readable outlined illustrations and cream paper; do not turn tasks into 3D puzzles |
| Map, wallet, speech/demo UI, drawing tools | 2D interface overlays | Same picture-led vocabulary and palette, with high-contrast focus/selection, accessible names and clear touch targets |

The current review uses generated scene stills and raster object cutouts as **design studies**. They are not a 3D engine, rigged characters or finished game assets. Production must build the world/interiors as 3D rather than use these backgrounds to claim a navigable 3D world.

## Visual language

Use honey timber, earthy terracotta, warm cream, leafy greens and restrained blue water. Keep the den's polished soft 3D shading and natural surface finish. Avoid the overly flat clay/plastic treatment rejected in v5, noisy photographic detail, sharply contrasting cinematic lighting and generic UI styling unrelated to the den.

Current interface tokens: ink `#4b3b2d`, green `#48663b`, paper `#fff5df`, honey `#e3b46b`, muted `#7c735d`. These are working design values, not a claim of completed accessibility validation. Puzzle cards are flat; world objects remain dimensional. Keep numerals/words only where they are learning content or useful accessible metadata, not required navigation prose.

## Interaction rules retained

One difficulty level; first-grade Zürich curriculum alignment; direct answer submission and automatic continuation after brief success feedback; syllable bows; non-punitive retry. The shop shows individual resource tokens on separate rows, with a compact price/wallet dock outside the shelf artwork. Purchase confirmation is separate from answering a puzzle.

## Revision policy

Version all art and review changes, preserve frozen references, compare new work against den and character master images, and always push completed project changes to GitHub main before reporting completion. The v7 art remains a candidate for user/child review, not a final approval of models or curriculum.
