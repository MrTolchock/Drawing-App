# Woodland picture expansion — 19 November 2026

150 new drawings in ten sheets extend the central library from 240 to 390 pictures. Every sheet follows `waldpost-watercolour-pencil-v1`, using the fixed animal and object reference pixels. Each has fifteen occupied cells in a 4 × 4 atlas; the last cell is transparent. Individual cells are 320 × 320 px, centred with the longest painted side at approximately 256 px.
