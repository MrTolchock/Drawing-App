# Third picture expansion — variety for future puzzles

150 new illustrations extend the library from 540 to 690 pictures in 49 atlases. The canonical `waldpost-watercolour-pencil-v1` reference pair defines every generation request. Ten new 4 × 4 atlases each contain fifteen pictures and one empty transparent cell. Runtime cells remain 320 px, with complete subjects normalized to about 256 px on their longest visible side.

[Browse and export individual PNGs](../../assets/waldatelier/index.html) · [Machine-readable subjects and puzzle uses](expansion-three-2026-11-19.json) · [Complete generator handoff](../artwork/generator-inputs.json)

The collection adds reusable visual structures for many puzzle rules. Spatial images keep relationships inside one vignette; state pairs differ visibly; making sequences share materials and products; quantities contain separate countable objects; patterned scarves keep one colour family so pattern recognition can be the task. Existing illustrations can supply the whole object, animal, finished product or matching tool.

## Examples for future authors

- **Spatial words:** `relation-apple-in-basket`, `relation-apple-above-basket`, `relation-apple-under-bench`, the viewer-left/right stump pair, and front/behind box pairs.
- **Opposites and states:** open/closed parcels, chests and locks; full/empty buckets; clean/muddy boots; whole/broken jug; folded/crumpled paper.
- **Sequences:** flour → dough → braided loaf; sheet → diagonal fold → paper boat; knitting start → half-finished → scarf; marked plank → cut boards → half-built birdhouse; washed berries → crushed berries → jam in a pot.
- **Materials and functions:** wool, linen, leather, felt, cardboard, glass, copper, slate and clay; hammer/nail, screwdriver/screw, wrench/nut/bolt, mortar/pestle.
- **Association and inference:** pair animal signs with existing animal pictures; pair acorn cap with `acorn`, walnut kernel/shell with `walnut`, apple seed with `apple`, and cherry stone with `cherry`.
- **Quantities:** blue buttons, acorns and maple leaves in exact groups of 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6. `family` and `quantity` are explicit in the subject plan. These support counting, matching equal quantities, comparing and simple arithmetic stories.
- **Visual patterns and cycles:** six blue/cream scarf patterns, four seasonal apple trees, and meadow, riverbank, alpine-rock, deadwood and hedgerow habitats.

Search the stable ID or German label, or choose its `expansion-…` sheet in the library. Use `FK.visual.art(id, className, accessibleLabel)` for a later puzzle. Existing puzzle definitions, reviews, original pictures and cell positions are preserved. No new puzzles are introduced in this artwork release.

Actual complete generator arguments, ordered reference hashes, raw PNGs and repair requests are retained. Each sheet has a specific forward prompt and packing manifest; the selected source and any explicit crop rectangles are recorded there.
