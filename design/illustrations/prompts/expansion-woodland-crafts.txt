WALDPOST CANONICAL ILLUSTRATION STYLE — waldpost-watercolour-pencil-v1

Use the supplied canonical animals.png and objects.png reference images as the authority for drawing technique. They show the predominant style of the current Waldpost gallery. Match their actual pixels, not an imagined style suggested by a filename.

Draw warm children's picture-book watercolour finished with visible coloured-pencil strokes. Use light, gently uneven washes, organic sepia contours, delicate hatching, softly irregular edges and modest drawn shading. Surfaces look painted and pencilled, with paper grain within the illustration. Keep the warmth of cream, honey brown, restrained sage and blue, while preserving every exact colour required by a task. Subjects remain friendly, recognizable and anatomically believable.

Do not turn the drawing into a 3D render, plush toy, clay or felt craft, photograph, glossy object, embossed surface or studio-lit miniature. Avoid deep ambient-occlusion shadows, inflated forms and realistic material microtexture. Do not mix in another illustration technique or use historical artwork as a style reference.

The role instructions below set the necessary detail and composition within this one style. Learning pictures retain fine drawn contours; small navigation symbols use the approved simpler, broader shapes and flat fills in the same palette. Keep the existing rendered game-character identities separate from image-generated learning subjects. Exact geometry and handwriting remain authored paths.

Preserve subject identity, counted quantities, exact answer colours and action differences. Use real alpha for isolated pictures and stationery, and an opaque canvas only when the selected role requires a scene or branding image. No accidental background, halo, visible grid, labels or extra objects.
