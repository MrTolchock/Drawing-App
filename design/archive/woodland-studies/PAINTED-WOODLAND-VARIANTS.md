# Painted woodland: two refinements of A

> Archived design/review note. Implementation and approval statements describe the stage when it was written. See the [current app notes](../../../archive/project-baseline/docs/HEART-TREE.md) and [design index](../../../archive/project-baseline/design/README.md) for the present build.

Archived exploration. After reviewing these variants, the user selected **the original A, Painted woodland**. Preserve its original balance of detail and open space; see the [chosen art direction](../../../archive/project-baseline/design/heart-tree/ART-DIRECTION.md). Work remains visual design only.

[View A1 and A2 — map above, future 3D scene below](painted-woodland-variants.png).

| Option | Change from A | Retained qualities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A1. Richer detail** | Add restrained moss, leaf groups, painted bark, roots and bank stones around landmarks. | Similar tree spacing and open ground; soft forms, clear paths and local areas of detail. |
| **A2. Closer woodland** | Bring small tree and hazel groups closer together, reducing empty grass between places and making the woodland more sheltered. | A's simpler surface treatment; broad clear routes and room around characters and interaction targets. |

The distinction is **local detail versus the spacing of vegetation**. A2 should feel more enclosed through grouped broad shapes, rather than through a dense layer of small decorative marks. Neither needs to return to the original forest's visual noise.

Both retain warm painted surfaces, Swiss-native plants and animals, the orange fox with green neckerchief and the existing story and place relationships. The map remains simpler than the close world view. Reading is not required to distinguish places, and a growing puzzle library does not add a field of map markers.

The [previous A/B comparison](visual-middle-ground-options.png) remains the reference for the base style. These new images are generated art-direction concepts, not implemented Three.js scenes. The flat puzzle artwork and app code remain unchanged.
